# DoneNow release checklist

This internal checklist is intentionally kept outside the app's Sources directory so it is not shipped in the product bundle.

- Configure the App Store bundle identifier and signing team.
- Register and sandbox-test the verified Pro product in App Store Connect.
- Add the public privacy-policy and support URLs and complete App Privacy disclosures.
- Complete physical-device, iPad, accessibility, audio, haptics, offline, and StoreKit tests.
